# Careers in Cyber — TryHackMe

**Difficulty:** Info  
**Category:** Career Development  
**Completed:** May 2026  

## Overview
An exploration of the different career paths available 
in the cybersecurity industry.

## What I Learned
- The wide range of cybersecurity roles that exist
- The difference between red team (offensive) and blue team 
  (defensive) careers
- What each role does day-to-day

## Roles Covered

| Role | What they do |
|------|-------------|
| Penetration Tester | Legally hack systems to find vulnerabilities |
| Red Teamer | Simulate full attacker scenarios for organizations |
| SOC Analyst | Monitor alerts and respond to threats in real time |
| Security Engineer | Build and maintain secure systems |
| Malware Analyst | Reverse-engineer malicious software |
| DFIR Analyst | Investigate security incidents after they happen |

## Key Takeaway
Cybersecurity is not one job — it's an entire ecosystem of 
specialized roles. This room helped me understand that my 
interest lies in both the analytical side (SOC/DFIR) and 
the technical offensive side (penetration testing).
